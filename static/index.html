BID / ASK — sell price / buy price.
You always buy at the higher one. The gap is SPR, your cost. Red = too wide to trade.
MID — what the market believes.
MODEL — what we believe.
Both are probabilities of the first-listed outcome (Over / that team).
SIDE — which way to bet if you took it.
These are yes/no contracts, so betting UNDER is just selling OVER.
EDGE — model minus what you'd pay.
Green = model thinks it's cheap. ? = disagreement over 15%, which usually means the model is wrong, not that it's free money.
SHADOW — the order we would have placed.
REST = sits on the book and earns a rebate. Nothing is ever actually sent.
