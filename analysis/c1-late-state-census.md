## What this means for round 3

The mechanism's tradable form exists on this venue: episodes occur at the
staked rate, books survive, and a tradable price band is present in ~4 of 10
rung-instants. Its economics do NOT exist at WNBA microstructure: the 12¢
median spread dwarfs any plausible curve error. The question the NBA forward
test answers, with the atlas empirical curves and day-one depth recording:
are NBA late-state totals books tight enough that the measured bimodal tail
mispricing — if the venue exhibits it at all — clears the toll. Register
nothing until that reads.

*n = 31 games, and every sub-cut above is 1–6 games. Composition, not
frequencies. No in-sample result justifies capital. The forward test is the
evidence.*
