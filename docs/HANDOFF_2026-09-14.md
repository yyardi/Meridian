## 2. What I got wrong, because it will save you the same mistakes

Four of my own claims were corrected by agents tonight, each caught by a
measurement rather than an argument. They are the most useful content here.

- **I registered a line that could not be stronger than its source.** The CFB
  50–60¢ fade is the exact complement of the cell that motivated it — same 227
  rows, other side — so its expectation is fixed by arithmetic at +7.45¢
  [−2.67, +17.57], spanning zero. A fade inherits its source's error bars and
  pays the round trip.
- **My decision rule had a clause that could not fire.** "The home/away twin
  does not contradict" required the twin below −9.62¢; it sits at +1.97¢. It
  passed on every achievable outcome. Retired to a reported number.
- **I claimed table tennis gives a powered read by Tuesday.** Wrong variance:
  the per-bet SD is the outcome's ~50¢, not the spread's. ~300 matches detects
  6.47¢ against a 2.00–2.50¢ hurdle. TT rules out LARGE edges in days and cannot
  establish a small one in under a fortnight.
- **I explained a coverage warning with live games and proposed subtracting
  them.** Refuted by one case where a live event appeared on both sides and
  cancelled; the fix would have been wrong while looking right.

Also mine: I ordered a cherry-pick from a branch whose diff was 3,752 insertions
against 18,900 deletions (it would have reverted a week), told an agent to stop a
query that had already finished, and asked twice for a remote-branch deletion
that is the operator's call. Each refusal came with numbers, which is why they
were cheap. **Ask agents to refuse with a measurement.**
